Frypan

I LIED. IM SO SORRY. HE'S STILL HERE.

Frypan is the keeper of the Cooks, boys who feed the population of 50 within the Glade, the job is demanding as all of the boys do some sort of manual labor meaning they eat a lot. Cooks also take care of the dishes along side the sloppers.(janitors) Cooks also work closely with the jobs within the Blood House and Track Hoes.

In the book hes discriped as stokey and hairy with dark skin, discribed by others as 'ill tempered', he's very caring and considerate, able to see situations from both points of views.
A big inside joke they all have is how ass his food is but in reality they all deeply enjoy his meals and appreciate him and his team cooking for them.
He's also very sentimental, reminising about the Glade after they had escaped and choosing to get the brain chip (that suppresses memories) removed after they had all agreed to keep them in.
(His real name was Toby. WICKED named him Siggy. The name Frypan was a nickname by the Gladers).
In the films he's shown to be quite proficient with heavy machinery, able to opporate cranes and large trucks, as well as being a marksman, he's often the first to pick up a gun and defend the people behind him. His main weapon is a shotgun. seen being used in many scenes.

Minho


Minho is the keeper of the Runners, boys that run out into the maze every morning the moment the doors open to memorize the changing paths and run back at sunset before the doors close. Needing to recover quickly, be sharp minded, have a good memory, and most importantly be fast and have good stamina.

Introduced in The Maze Runner as an asian boy with strong arms, his first appearance was in the book 'The Feaver Code' showing him as a boy fighting the nurses and screaming warnings to other patients before they can sedate him for a brain chip implant surgery.
As a character he's very charismatic and whitty, often poking fun at Thomas and making snarky remarks but still being likeable due to his reliability and leadership skills. He takes pride in being a Runner, and we can see his endurance being put to the test during the film version of 'The Death Cure where he was kiddnapped while defending the survivors camp and tourmented with memories of The Maze and constant tests to extract a liquid cure from his brains stimulation.

Winston


Another overlooked character!!! Winston is keeper of the Slicers and the BloodHouse, the Slicers raise and kill the animals, then the BloodHouse butchers them and prepares the meat before handing it off to the Cooks. Not only do they work with the Cooks to provide protein full food to the entire Glade, they also work with the TrackHoes, they do the heavy lifting in the Gardens and therefore give a hand in repairing animal pens.
